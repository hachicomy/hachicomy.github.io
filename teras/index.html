The Core

Named for the heart
of the matter.

Teras (Malay, noun) means the core, and the heartwood of a tree: the dense centre that holds everything up.

Our mark is a core sample, the most trusted physical evidence in the industry. Its three strata mark the journey every volume takes through Teras.

  1. speed 01

    Measured

    Meter readings, well tests, tank dips and lab analyses arrive from the field, historians and files. Each is checked at the door, so problems are caught before they reach a report.

  2. account_tree 02

    Allocated

    A calculation engine allocates fiscal volumes back to wells, fields, licences and partners, daily and monthly, using rules your engineers define and can inspect.

  3. task 03

    Reported

    Approved figures close the period and flow to regulatory, partner and management reports, with the full trail from report line back to meter.

Under the Core

Engineered for
precision and control.

Built from the ground up for today's operations: a modern core, open standards and a small footprint that runs as well in a customer's data centre as in the cloud.

Your cloud Your data centre Single-tenant
Calculation engine
Written in Rust for speed and predictable results. The same engine checks formulas while they are written and runs production allocations.
Data platform
PostgreSQL, with effective-dated master data, cell-level change history and conflict-safe concurrent editing.
Deployment
Container images for any major cloud or your own data centre. Storage and email use open interfaces, with no dependency on a single provider.
Security & access
Single sign-on with OpenID Connect, role-based permissions, four-eyes approval and a complete audit trail.
Units & precision
Field, SI or your own unit set, with managed, date-effective conversions. Full stored precision is always one click away.
Live & observable
Screens update as data changes. Tracing with OpenTelemetry follows every request and background job end to end.
Scale
Designed for a year of daily data across dozens of wells and columns in a single view, without paging.
Accessibility
Built to WCAG 2.2 AA, with full keyboard operation for data-dense work.
