The Risk Committee

Strategic Considerations.

What does "Bring Your Own Cloud" actually mean?

We deploy the GAJAH Stack directly into your AWS, Azure, or on-premises environment. You retain full data sovereignty and pay your cloud provider directly at raw compute costs. Hachico does not host your data; we maintain a secure, encrypted management tunnel to keep the system healthy, ensuring zero vendor lock-in to an external hosting platform.

What is Hachico's "Exit Strategy"?

This is our strongest differentiator. Because we build on standard open-source PostgreSQL and deploy infrastructure directly in your account, you retain complete control. If you ever decide to end our contract, you keep everything. We hand over the encryption keys and administrative credentials. Your database continues running without interruption. No migration required. Zero technical debt in leaving.

How does GAJAH compare to AWS RDS or Aurora?

AWS RDS and Aurora charge a massive managed-service premium and severely limit low-level database control. The GAJAH Stack runs on raw EC2 or on-premises compute, frequently reducing infrastructure costs by 40–60%. Furthermore, it grants your engineers access to advanced extensions, host-level observability, and kernel tuning impossible in standard RDS environments.

How do you mitigate key-person ("bus factor") risk?

Relying on a single internal DBA creates a critical single point of failure. Hachico eliminates this by combining self-healing cluster automation with dedicated, fractional database engineering oversight. We codify institutional knowledge into code (IaC) and operational practices—giving you an entire reliability team for a fraction of the cost of a single senior hire.
